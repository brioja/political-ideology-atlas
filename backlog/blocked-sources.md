# Audits held back by sources we couldn't reach

Audits whose result was limited by access, not by the subject's record: sites that refuse automated
downloads (403/503), texts only on loan or behind a paywall, failed downloads. A retry can change the
result once the text is reached another way (a maintainer download, a library copy, a book supplied in
`~/Documents/ideology reference/<Name>/`, a mirror). Each entry names what was blocked and what the
audit lacks because of it. Remove an entry once a later pass reaches the sources.

Audit agents list every source they could not open in their report; the integrator adds an entry here
when that gap plausibly cost answers (not for a reference page that had other copies).
