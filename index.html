Eigencompass

Most political tests decide the axes first and then sort you onto them. This test works the other way round. It writes out how 19 ideologies would answer each statement, then uses principal component analysis to find the directions in which they actually disagree. Those directions become the axes, and the data decides how many of them matter.

At the end you get your nearest ideologies, how close each one is, and the specific statements that pulled you toward or away from them.
